## 3. Taxonomy governance

- Confirm categories against actual stock and business priorities.
- Avoid near-duplicate categories and confusing synonyms.
- Assign each product one or more appropriate categories.
- Use a canonical primary category for breadcrumbs and product URL context if needed.
- Keep category names and descriptions translated.
- Do not publish empty categories unless they have useful explanatory content and a business reason.
- Plan redirects before renaming/removing indexed categories.

## 4. Product content checklist

For each product, gather:
- Accurate product name.
- SKU, if used.
- Brand, if applicable.
- Approved primary and gallery images.
- Short and full description.
- Genuine specifications and units.
- Relevant category assignments.
- Variant information, if applicable.
- Price display policy and verified price, if shown.
- Availability status, only if maintained.
- English and Urdu name/description.
- SEO title and description per locale.
- Alt text for informative images.

## 5. Editorial and testimonial policy

- Blog articles must be original, useful, fact-checked, and reviewed.
- Testimonials/reviews must be genuine and have publication permission.
- Do not generate fictional customer identities or reviews.
